Let's find out what AI your business is actually using — then walk through the one that worries you most.

First, a quick checklist (about 2-3 minutes): AI tools people use directly (like ChatGPT or Claude), AI features already built into apps you pay for (like Salesforce or Microsoft 365), and any custom systems your team may have built. Most businesses find more than they expected.

Then we'll ask which one concerns you most, and walk through it with you (about 15 minutes): what it can see, what it can do, what happens if it's wrong, and what's stopping that from turning into a real problem. You'll come out the other end with a decision, not just a list.

Don't know an answer? That's normal, not a failure — we'll tell you exactly who to ask and what to say.
