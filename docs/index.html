Why This Matters

🚨 The Problem

Companies are deploying Claude and ChatGPT without governance. One tired engineer at 3am can break everything.

⚠️ The Risk

AI might execute production commands with escalated privileges. No audit trail. No clarity who did what. Catastrophic failures waiting to happen.

✅ The Solution

Guardrails makes governance easy. 45-minute workflow. Customizable policy. Tool recommendations. Works for startups and enterprises.

  • Policy Templates
